# 📌 FairWork Setu — Project Executive Summary & Vision

## 1. Project Identity & Purpose
* **Project Name:** Setu (सेतु — meaning *"Bridge"* in Hindi) / FairWork Setu
* **Tagline:** *"Aapke Kaam, Paisa, Sehat aur Skill ka Sathi"* (Your Companion for Work, Money, Health, and Skills)
* **Domain:** Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning applied to Social Good / Informal Gig Economy
* **Academic Level:** 5th Semester Mini Project (B.E. / B.Tech Computer Science & AIML)

---

## 2. Problem Statement: The Reality of Indian Gig Workers
India is home to an estimated **450 million unorganized and informal workers**, including over **12 million active platform gig workers** (delivery partners on Swiggy/Zomato/Blinkit, ride-hailing drivers on Ola/Uber, domestic workers on Urban Company, freelance electricians, plumbers, construction labourers, and street vendors).

Unlike formal salaried professionals, gig workers face acute socioeconomic precarity:
1. **Cash Flow Volatility:** Earnings fluctuate violently day-to-day based on platform algorithms, surge multipliers, customer volume, weather, and fuel price swings.
2. **Lack of Financial Literacy & Budgeting Tools:** Most mainstream fintech apps (CRED, Mint, Excel) are designed for monthly salaried workers with bank statements, leaving cash-and-UPI daily-wage earners behind.
3. **Low Digital Literacy & Language Barriers:** Workers speak diverse Indic vernaculars (Hindi, Marathi, etc.) and struggle with complex text-heavy English forms.
4. **Informal Experience Remains Unrecognized:** Years of high-skill plumbing, electrical, or cooking experience cannot be verified because workers lack formal resumes or accreditation.
5. **Welfare Disconnect:** Millions of Rupees in government welfare schemes (e.g., e-Shram, PM-JAY, PM-SVANidhi) go unclaimed because workers do not know they qualify or cannot parse government gazettes.

## 3. The Solution: What is FairWork Setu?
Setu is an **AI-driven, voice-first, vernacular livelihood companion** engineered to empower informal gig workers. It acts as an intelligent digital assistant spanning 4 interconnected life pillars:

```
                          ┌───────────────────────────┐
                          │     FAIRWORK SETU (सेतु)   │
                          └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                                        │
     ┌──────────────────┬───────────────┴───────────────┬──────────────────┐
     ▼                  ▼                               ▼                  ▼
┌───────────┐     ┌───────────┐                   ┌───────────┐     ┌───────────┐
│   काम     │     │    धन     │                   │   सेहत    │     │   कौशल    │
│  (Kaam)   │     │  (Dhan)   │                   │  (Sehat)  │     │ (Kaushal) │
│ Work & Pay│     │Financial  │                   │ Health &  │     │ Skill     │
│ Matching  │     │Intel & ML │                   │ Welfare   │     │ Passport  │
└───────────┘     └───────────┘                   └───────────┘     └───────────┘
```

### The 4 Core Pillars:
1. **💼 काम (Kaam — Fair Work & Opportunities):**
   * Transparent gig matching based on fair wages, location proximity, and worker skills.
   * Eliminates exploitative middleman cuts and opaque platform commission penalties.
2. **💰 धन (Dhan — Financial Intelligence & Forecasting):**
   * Daily income and expense tracking with one-tap voice input.
   * **ML Predictive Forecasting:** Predicts next-day net balance and warns of budget deficits before they occur.
   * **Interactive Financial Calendar:** Integrated calendar selector with day-by-day transaction views and local timezone consistency.
   * **Financial Health Score (0–100):** Actionable, non-punitive creditworthiness and savings index.
3. **🩺 सेहत (Sehat — Health & Social Protection):**
   * Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) driven welfare eligibility checker for government social security schemes (e-Shram, Ayushman Bharat, BOCW).
   * Fatigue monitoring and hydration/rest reminders for high-stress delivery riders and drivers.
4. **📜 कौशल (Kaushal — Skills & Portable Identity):**
   * Automatic extraction of work experience into a verifiable **Digital Skill Passport**.
   * Generates a sharable, QR-coded credential and recommends localized upskilling courses.

## 4. Voice-First & Vernacular Inclusion
Setu is built around the premise that **voice is the primary interface for India's unorganized workforce**.
* **Multilingual Speech-to-Text & Information Extraction:** Workers speak natural sentences in Hindi, Marathi, or English (e.g., *"आज स्विगी से 650 रुपये मिले और 150 का पेट्रोल भरवाया"*).
* **Indic Numeral & Word Recognition:** Handles written and spoken numerals in Devanagari script (`१००`, `५००`), native number words (*"दोनशे"*, *"पांच सौ"*, *"हज़ार"*), and conversational currency shorthand (*"सौ"*, *"ढाई सौ"*).
* **High Contrast, Clean Ergonomics:** Designed with large touch targets, tactile color-coded buttons, audio feedback, and high visual accessibility.

---

## 5. Academic Evaluation & Machine Learning Integration
To meet rigorous computer science academic requirements, Setu integrates **4 distinct core ML algorithms**:
1. **OLS Linear Regression:** Mathematical modeling of daily cash flows and feature importance.
2. **L2 Logistic Regression:** Probabilistic classification of budget deficit risk.
3. **Deep Learning PyTorch LSTM:** Temporal sequence modeling capturing weekly cyclicality and earnings momentum.
4. **Unsupervised K-Means Clustering:** Profiling workers into 4 distinct spending archetypes ($k=4$) for customized micro-savings nudges.

In addition, Setu includes an **Interactive AI Lab (`AiLabPage.tsx`)** that allows professors, evaluators, and students to train models, tweak hyperparameters, test synthetic scenarios, and inspect evaluation metrics in real time.
