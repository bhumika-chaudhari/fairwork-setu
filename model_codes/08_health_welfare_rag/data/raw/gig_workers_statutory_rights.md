## 1. Category 1: ID Deactivation, Account Suspension & Arbitrary Termination
- **Keywords & Dialect Phrases:**
  `deactivation`, `deactivated`, `account suspended`, `id blocked`, `account banned`, `unfair termination`, `low rating block`, `fake customer complaint`, `arbitrary dismissal`, `shadow ban`, `blacklisted`, `reinstate id`, `reactivate account`, `id block`, `id band ho gayi`, `account suspend kar diya`, `login nahi ho raha`, `bina notice nikal diya`, `rating kam hone se block`, `customer ki jhooti complaint par block`, `fraud bolkar id block kar di`, `warning nahi di`.
- **Governing Statutory Law:**
  - Section 13 & 14, *Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Act, 2023*.
  - Section 114, *Code on Social Security, 2020*.
  - Principles of Natural Justice (*Audi Alteram Partem* — Right to be heard).
- **Worker Statutory Rights:**
  1. **Mandatory 14-Day Notice:** Under Section 14 of the Rajasthan Gig Workers Act, no platform aggregator (e.g., Zomato, Swiggy, Uber, Ola, Zepto, Blinkit, Urban Company) may terminate, suspend, or block a gig worker's ID without providing at least **14 days mandatory written notice** stating the verifiable reasons.
  2. **Right to a Fair Hearing & Counter-Evidence:** The worker has the statutory right to present trip logs, GPS tracking records, customer chat screenshots, or counter-evidence before an Internal Dispute Redressal Committee prior to permanent deactivation.
  3. **Protection Against Rating-Only Terminations:** Algorithmic termination based purely on automated customer rating dips below an arbitrary threshold without human review violates principles of natural justice and fair labor contracts.
  4. **Appellate Tribunal Redressal:** If the aggregator fails to resolve the deactivation within 14 days, the worker has the statutory right to petition the **State Gig Workers Welfare Board Tribunal** and Appellate Authority. Aggregators violating these provisions face statutory fines of up to ₹5 Lakhs.
- **Escalation & Action:**
  - Launch the **Haq Setu Automated Reinstatement Wizard (`/haq#appeal-wizard`)** to draft an official legal notice to the aggregator's Grievance Officer.

## 2. Category 2: Wage Theft, Withheld Payouts & Arbitrary Deductions
- **Keywords & Dialect Phrases:**
  `wage theft`, `payout withheld`, `delayed salary`, `penalty deduction`, `arbitrary fine`, `incentive withheld`, `payment pending`, `unpaid wages`, `fuel deduction`, `trip fare reduction`, `tips not credited`, `wallet balance on hold`, `paisa nahi diya`, `paise kaat liye`, `payout atak gaya`, `hafta nahi mila`, `thekedar paise nahi de raha`, `incentive kaat liya`, `galat penalty laga di`, `fare deduction`, `tip kaat li`, `wallet hold pe hai`.
- **Governing Statutory Law:**
  - *The Payment of Wages Act, 1936*.
  - Section 114, *Code on Social Security, 2020*.
  - Ministry of Labour & Employment Fair Remuneration Directives.
- **Worker Statutory Rights:**
  1. **Illegal Wage Penalties:** Automatic deductions from weekly gig payouts labeled as "customer cancellation penalties", "transit delay charges", or "missing item fines" without verifiable documentary evidence of worker willful misconduct are unlawful under the Payment of Wages Act.
  2. **Mandatory Payout Cycles:** Contractual earnings, surged trip incentives, and customer tips must be disbursed according to designated weekly or fortnightly payment cycles. Withholding earned wallet balances without cause constitutes an actionable labor dispute.
  3. **Tip Protection:** 100% of voluntary tips provided by customers through digital payment gateways belong legally to the delivery partner and cannot be confiscated or used to offset platform fare minimums.
- **Escalation & Action:**
  - Issue a formal **Wage Recovery Demand Letter** via Haq Setu.
  - Lodge an online labor grievance on the Ministry of Labour’s **SAMADHAN Portal (`https://samadhan.labour.gov.in`)**.
  - Call the National Consumer Helpline at **1915** for unfair trade practices.

## 3. Category 3: Road Accidents & On-Duty Injury Compensation
- **Keywords & Dialect Phrases:**
  `road accident`, `shift injury`, `accidental death`, `hospitalization expenses`, `insurance claim rejected`, `permanent disability`, `on-duty crash`, `delivery accident claim`, `medical reimbursement`, `compensation amount`, `accident ho gaya`, `gadi lad gayi`, `chot lag gayi`, `shift ke time accident hua`, `hospital ka kharcha`, `bima ka paisa`, `insurance claim reject ho gaya`, `haddi toot gayi`, `death compensation`.
- **Governing Statutory Law:**
  - *The Employee's Compensation Act, 1923*.
  - Section 166, *The Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, 2019*.
  - State Platform Based Gig Workers Welfare Fund Rules.
- **Worker Statutory Rights:**
  1. **Mandatory Group Accidental Insurance:** Aggregators are legally required to provide comprehensive accidental insurance cover for all active delivery partners:
     - **₹5 Lakh to ₹10 Lakh** in case of accidental death or permanent total disability.
     - **Up to ₹1 Lakh** for inpatient hospitalization and emergency medical treatment.
  2. **Transit & Wait Interval Coverage:** Protection is not restricted solely to the minutes between pick-up and drop-off; insurance coverage applies across active shift duty—including waiting at restaurant clusters, refueling, and returning from deliveries.
  3. **Unlawful Claim Rejection:** Insurance partners cannot arbitrarily reject claims on technicalities (e.g., claiming the worker was "on break" during an active login shift).
- **Escalation & Action:**
  - File a Police First Information Report (FIR) or Station Diary entry within 24 hours.
  - Secure Hospital Admission and Discharge Summaries with original pharmacy receipts.
  - Submit a formal compensation claim within 30 days via Haq Setu's *Accident Claim Support* and petition the Commissioner for Employee's Compensation.

## 4. Category 4: Workplace Violence, Customer Abuse & Shift Safety
- **Keywords & Dialect Phrases:**
  `customer assaulted`, `verbal abuse`, `physical violence`, `gated society lift ban`, `elevator restriction`, `harassment of female worker`, `police seized bike`, `unlawful detention`, `threatened on delivery`, `safety sos`, `customer ne maara`, `gali galoj ki`, `maar-peet ho gayi`, `society guard ne roka`, `lift me nahi aane diya`, `mahila worker ke sath badtameezi`, `police ne gadi pakad li`, `chori ka ilzam lagaya`.
- **Governing Statutory Law:**
  - *Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023* / *IPC* Sections 115 (Voluntarily causing hurt), 351 (Criminal intimidation), 352 (Intentional insult with intent to provoke breach of peace).
  - *The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (PoSH Act)*.
  - Constitutional Right to Dignity (Article 21) & Public Utility Access.
- **Worker Statutory Rights:**
  1. **Protection Against Physical Assault & Abuse:** Physical assault, verbal intimidation, or wrongful confinement by customers or security staff is a cognizable criminal offense. In immediate danger, workers must immediately dial **112 (Emergency Police)**.
  2. **Illegality of Gated Society Passenger Lift Bans:** Residential Welfare Associations (RWAs) and apartment societies cannot legally bar delivery riders or domestic workers from using passenger elevators. Arbitrary elevator bans based on class or trade violate constitutional dignity under Article 21 and municipal public utility regulations.
  3. **Right to a Zero FIR:** Any police station in India is legally obligated under Supreme Court mandates to register a **Zero FIR** for cognizable offenses (violence, severe assault), regardless of whether the incident occurred in their jurisdiction.
  4. **PoSH Protection for Female Gig Workers:** Women delivery partners and home service professionals are protected under the PoSH Act 2013, with access to Local Complaint Committees (LCC) in every district.
- **Escalation & Action:**
  - Call **112** immediately for on-scene police protection.
  - Record dated video/audio evidence and vehicle numbers.
  - Lodge a formal complaint with the local Station House Officer (SHO) and State Human Rights Commission.

## 5. Category 5: Contractor / Subcontractor Fraud & EPF/ESIC Embezzlement
- **Keywords & Dialect Phrases:**
  `contractor fraud`, `subcontractor withheld wages`, `pf not deposited in epfo`, `esic card not issued`, `agency took security deposit`, `unregistered agency`, `illegal commission cut`, `abrupt dismissal by agency`, `thekedar ne dhoka diya`, `agency wale paise nahi de rahe`, `commission zyada kaat rahe hain`, `PF ka paisa jama nahi kiya`, `esic nahi banwaya`, `security deposit wapas nahi diya`.
- **Governing Statutory Law:**
  - Section 21, *The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970*.
  - *The Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952*.
  - Section 406 & 409, *Indian Penal Code (Criminal Breach of Trust)*.
- **Worker Statutory Rights:**
  1. **Principal Employer Strict Liability:** Under Section 21(4) of the Contract Labour Act, if an intermediate labor contractor, staffing agency, or vendor fails to disburse wages, the **Principal Employer** (the main operating company) is legally obligated to pay the entire unpaid wages directly to the worker.
  2. **Criminal Liability for EPF Embezzlement:** If a contractor deducts EPF (Provident Fund) or ESIC from a worker's monthly earnings but fails to deposit it into the government EPFO treasury within 15 days, it constitutes criminal breach of trust under IPC 406/409, punishable by imprisonment.
  3. **Illegal Security Deposits:** Deducting non-refundable security deposits as a prerequisite for onboarding informal workers is an unlawful practice under state labor welfare regulations.
- **Escalation & Action:**
  - File an online complaint on **EPFiGMS (`https://epfigms.gov.in`)** citing contractor details and UAN.
  - Submit a joint petition to the Regional Labour Commissioner citing Section 21 of the Contract Labour Act.

## 6. Category 6: Government Welfare Registration & Worker Cards (e-Shram & BOCW)
- **Keywords & Dialect Phrases:**
  `e-shram benefits`, `bocw card registration`, `shramik card benefits`, `gig worker unique id`, `welfare board registration`, `children scholarship for laborers`, `maternity grant for informal workers`, `pmsby accidental cover`, `e-shram card ke fayde`, `bocw card kaise banaye`, `shramik card`, `gig worker id kaise milegi`, `welfare board me panjikaran`, `bacchon ki scholarship`, `shramik aawas yojana`.
- **Governing Statutory Law:**
  - Section 113, *The Code on Social Security, 2020*.
  - *The Building and Other Construction Workers (BOCW) Welfare Cess Act, 1996*.
- **Worker Statutory Rights:**
  1. **e-Shram Card & Accidental Protection:** Every registered unorganised and gig worker receives a 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN) with automatic ₹2 Lakh accidental death and permanent disability insurance under Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY), plus nationwide social security portability.
  2. **BOCW Welfare Board Grants:** Registered construction and informal trade workers receive:
     - **Children's Education Scholarships:** ₹8,000 to ₹25,000 per year from class 1 through higher university education.
     - **Maternity Assistance:** ₹21,000 financial grant for registered female workers.
     - **Daughter's Marriage Grant:** ₹51,000 to ₹1,00,000 financial assistance.
     - **Housing Repair & Tool Assistance:** Subsidized loans and ₹5,000 tool purchase grants.
- **Escalation & Action:**
  - Self-enroll at `https://eshram.gov.in` using Aadhaar and mobile OTP.
  - Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or district labor office for BOCW worker booklet endorsement.

## 7. Category 7: Formal Appeal Drafting & Legal Notice Escalation Protocol
- **Keywords & Dialect Phrases:**
  `draft appeal letter`, `legal notice to company`, `complaint to labour commissioner`, `appeal format`, `arbitration notice`, `grievance redressal application`, `formal appeal template`, `appeal kaise kare`, `legal notice bhejna hai`, `labour commissioner ko complaint kaise likhe`, `grievance form`, `shikayat patra draft kar do`.
- **4-Stage Legal Escalation Protocol:**
  1. **Stage 1 (Internal In-App Dispute):** Log an official dispute ticket within the delivery partner application. Capture timestamped screenshots of customer chats, trip routes, wallet balances, and note the Ticket ID.
  2. **Stage 2 (Statutory Legal Notice):** If unaddressed or denied after 48 hours, issue a formal statutory notice to the platform aggregator's designated Grievance Officer citing Section 14 of the Rajasthan Gig Workers Act and Section 114 of the Code on Social Security.
  3. **Stage 3 (Welfare Board Tribunal Petition):** If unresolved after 14 days, file an electronic petition before the State Gig Workers Welfare Board Tribunal seeking binding reinstatement and lost-wage compensation.
  4. **Stage 4 (Appellate Conciliation & Labour Court):** Appeal to the State Labour Commissionerate for formal statutory conciliation and prosecution of aggregator non-compliance.
- **Escalation & Action:**
  - Access the **Haq Setu Automated Reinstatement Wizard (`/haq#appeal-wizard`)** to automatically generate an editable, legally sound legal notice in English and Hindi.
