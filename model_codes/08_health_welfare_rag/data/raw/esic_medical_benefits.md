# Employees' State Insurance Scheme (ESIC) Medical & Sickness Benefits

- **Issuing Organization:** Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC), Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
- **Official Portal:** https://www.esic.gov.in
- **Toll-Free Helpline:** 1800-11-2526 / 14470
- **Category:** Social Security, Healthcare & Sickness Cash Benefit
- **Last Updated:** August 2023

## Scheme Overview
The Employees' State Insurance Act provides social security and health protection to workers in organized and transitional sectors against contingencies of sickness, maternity, disablement, and death due to employment injury. Under recent national social security initiatives (Code on Social Security 2020), ESIC benefits are being progressively extended to platform gig workers and gig delivery aggregators.

## Medical Care Benefits
- **Full Medical Care:** Comprehensive outpatient and inpatient medical care provided to insured workers (IP - Insured Persons) and their dependent family members from day one of insurable employment.
- **Facilities:** Services delivered through dedicated ESI dispensaries, 160+ ESIC Model Hospitals, and tie-up super-specialty hospitals for tertiary treatments (cardiology, oncology, nephrology).
- **No Ceiling on Medical Expenses:** There is no financial cap on the medical care expenses incurred for the treatment of an Insured Person or their family members.

## Cash Benefits for Sickness and Disablement
1. **Sickness Benefit:**
   - 70% of the average daily wage payable during certified medical sickness for up to 91 days in any continuous two consecutive benefit periods.
   - Requires payment of contributions for not less than 78 days in the corresponding contribution period.
2. **Extended Sickness Benefit:**
   - 80% of daily wage for up to 2 years for 34 specified long-term debilitating diseases (such as tuberculosis, cancer, mental illness, heart diseases).
3. **Temporary Disablement Benefit (TDB):**
   - 90% of daily wage paid continuously from day one of employment injury or occupational accident resulting in temporary disability, until recovery or stabilization.
4. **Permanent Disablement Benefit (PDB):**
   - Monthly pension paid at up to 90% of wage depending on the percentage loss of earning capacity determined by the Medical Board.
5. **Maternity Benefit:**
   - 100% of average daily wage for 26 weeks for female insured workers during confinement/childbirth.

## Relevance to Platform & Gig Workers
- Aggregators participating in ESIC pilot initiatives register delivery partners and drivers via their e-Shram UAN (Universal Account Number).
- Once registered, gig workers receive an e-Pehchan card granting access to nearby ESIC dispensaries and emergency accident care without hospital fees.
