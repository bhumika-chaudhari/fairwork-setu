# Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

- **Issuing Organization:** National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India
- **Official Portal:** https://pmjay.gov.in / https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in
- **National Helpline:** 14555 / 1800-111-565
- **Category:** Health Insurance & Hospitalization Coverage
- **Last Updated:** October 2023

## Scheme Overview
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government-funded health assurance scheme. It provides a health cover of up to ₹5,00,000 (Five Lakh Rupees) per eligible family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization. The scheme is completely cashless and paperless at all empanelled public and private healthcare providers across India.

## Key Benefits
- **Financial Protection:** Up to ₹5 lakh per family per year on a family floater basis, with no restriction on family size, age, or gender.
- **Coverage Details:** Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization expenses, including diagnostic tests, room charges, ICU fees, physician consultations, medical implants, surgery, and medications.
- **Pre-existing Conditions:** All pre-existing medical conditions are covered from day one of enrollment without any waiting period.
- **Cashless Treatment:** Beneficiaries do not have to pay any advance deposit or out-of-pocket expenses at empanelled healthcare providers (EHCP).
- **Portability:** Benefits can be availed at any empanelled hospital (public or private) anywhere in India, allowing migrant and gig workers to access healthcare away from their home state.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Identified based on deprivation and occupational criteria from the Socio-Economic Caste Census 2011 (SECC 2011) for rural and urban areas.
- Urban informal workers automatically covered include:
  - Street vendors, hawkers, cobblers, and ragpickers.
  - Domestic workers, cleaners, sanitation workers, and sweepers.
  - Transport workers, drivers, conductors, delivery gig workers, and rickshaw pullers.
  - Construction workers, plumbers, masons, painters, and electricians.
  - Shop workers, tailors, mechanics, repair workers, and artisans.
- Eligible families possessing Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) or Priority Household (PHH) ration cards under NFSA.

## How Informal & Gig Workers Can Access Benefits
1. **Check Eligibility:** Visit https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in, use the Ayushman App, or call 14555 with your Aadhaar number or Ration card number.
2. **Ayushman Card Generation:** Complete Aadhaar e-KYC at any Common Service Center (CSC), Primary Health Center (PHC), or empanelled hospital through the Pradhan Mantri Arogya Mitra (PMAM).
3. **Hospital Admission:** Present your Ayushman Card or Aadhaar number at the PM-JAY helpdesk at any empanelled hospital for instant verification and cashless admission.
