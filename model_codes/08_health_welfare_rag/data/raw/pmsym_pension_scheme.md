# Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan (PM-SYM) Pension Scheme

- **Issuing Organization:** Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
- **Implementing Agency:** Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) & Common Service Centres (CSC e-Governance Services India Ltd.)
- **Official Portal:** https://maandhan.in
- **National Helpdesk:** 1800-267-6888
- **Category:** Old-Age Pension & Social Security for Unorganised Workers
- **Launched:** February 2019

## Scheme Overview
PM-SYM is a central government-sponsored voluntary and contributory pension scheme meant for ensuring old-age income security to unorganised and informal workers, including gig delivery workers, domestic workers, street vendors, rickshaw drivers, construction workers, and agricultural labourers.

## Key Financial Benefits
- **Assured Monthly Pension:** ₹3,000 per month guaranteed lifelong pension once the beneficiary completes 60 years of age.
- **Family Pension on Death:** If the subscriber dies during the receipt of the pension, their spouse is entitled to receive 50% of the pension (₹1,500/month) as family pension.
- **Government Matching Contribution:** The scheme operates on a 50:50 basis. For every rupee deposited by the worker into their pension account (₹55 to ₹200 per month depending on age of entry), the Central Government deposits an equal matching amount.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Must be an unorganised or informal worker (such as auto driver, courier/delivery rider, tailor, street hawker, domestic helper).
- Entry age: Between 18 and 40 years.
- Monthly income: ₹15,000 per month or below.
- Must not be covered under other formal statutory schemes:
  - Not an existing member of EPFO, NPS, or ESIC.
  - Not an income tax assessee.
- Required documents: Aadhaar card and Savings Bank / Jan Dhan account with auto-debit facility.

## Contribution Table by Age
- Age 18: ₹55/month worker share + ₹55/month government share = ₹110 total
- Age 25: ₹80/month worker share + ₹80/month government share = ₹160 total
- Age 30: ₹105/month worker share + ₹105/month government share = ₹210 total
- Age 35: ₹150/month worker share + ₹150/month government share = ₹300 total
- Age 40: ₹200/month worker share + ₹200/month government share = ₹400 total

## How to Enroll
1. Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or visit https://maandhan.in for self-enrollment.
2. Provide Aadhaar Card and Savings/Jan Dhan Bank details (IFSC code and account number).
3. The initial contribution amount is paid in cash at the CSC center, and subsequent monthly installments are auto-debited from the bank account.
4. An instant Shram Yogi Pension Card with a unique Shram Yogi Pension Account Number is generated.
