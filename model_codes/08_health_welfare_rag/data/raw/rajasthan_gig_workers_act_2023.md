# Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers (Registration and Welfare) Act, 2023

- **Issuing Organization:** Department of Labour, Government of Rajasthan
- **Official Status:** State Legislative Act (Act No. 17 of 2023)
- **Official Portal:** https://labour.rajasthan.gov.in
- **Category:** Gig Worker Social Security & Emergency Assistance
- **Enacted:** July 2023

## Act Overview
The Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers (Registration and Welfare) Act, 2023 is India's first landmark legislation designed specifically to protect the legal rights, social security, occupational health, and welfare of app-based gig workers (delivery partners, rideshare drivers, home service workers, and quick-commerce agents).

## The Gig Workers Welfare Board & Fund
- **Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Welfare Board:**
  - Tripartite board comprising government officials, representatives of gig worker unions/associations, and platform aggregator representatives (e.g., Zomato, Swiggy, Uber, Ola, Urban Company, Zepto, Blinkit).
- **The Social Security and Welfare Fund:**
  - Financed primarily by a dedicated **Welfare Cess** (ranging between 1% and 2% per customer transaction) levied on platform aggregators operating within the state.
  - Contributions are deposited directly into a designated government-managed fund and cannot be deducted from worker payouts or fares.

## Welfare & Health Benefits for Gig Workers
- **Accident & Emergency Medical Relief:** Immediate cashless hospitalization and compensation for road accidents, physical assaults, or severe health emergencies occurring during active shifts or delivery routes.
- **Health Insurance & Preventive Health Camps:** Subsidized or fully funded health insurance packages tailored for the occupational hazards of gig workers (ergonomic strain, pollution exposure, traffic injuries).
- **Unique Worker ID (UID):**
  - Every registered gig worker is assigned a permanent, unique registration ID that remains valid across multiple platforms and apps.
  - Portability ensures that switching aggregators or working for two platforms simultaneously does not disrupt accumulated social security benefits.
- **Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Account Deactivation Protection:**
  - **Section 13 & 14 (Mandatory Notice Before Deactivation):** No platform aggregator can deactivate, blacklist, or terminate a gig worker's account arbitrarily or without due notice.
  - Aggregators are legally required to provide at least **14 days mandatory written notice** stating clear, verifiable reasons before deactivating or suspending a worker's account or app access.
  - The gig worker has the statutory right to be heard and submit an explanation within the 14-day window.
  - Workers can file an appeal against unlawful deactivation or arbitrary deductions with the **Appellate Grievance Redressal Officer and Welfare Board Tribunal**, which must decide cases within a time-bound statutory deadline.
  - Aggregators violating these deactivation provisions are subject to statutory financial penalties ranging up to ₹5 Lakhs for repeated contraventions under the Act.

## Worker Registration Process
1. Aggregators must automatically onboard all onboarded gig workers operating within Rajasthan into the state portal within 60 days.
2. Gig workers can independently verify or update their profile using their Aadhaar and mobile number on the Rajasthan Labour portal.
3. Access to welfare fund grants and emergency medical reimbursement is initiated via the worker's unique ID.
